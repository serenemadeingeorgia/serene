About SERENE

The Concept

SERENE is a Georgia-based atelier specializing in premium loungewear. Founded on the principle of intentional living, we create garments that bridge the gap between private comfort and sartorial elegance.

Craftsmanship

Every piece is designed and produced entirely in Georgia, collaborating with local master seamstresses to ensure absolute oversight of every stitch.

Materials

Exclusively high-grade silk and velvet, selected for structural integrity, superior drape, and tactile longevity.

Slow Production

We operate on a slow-release model, producing in limited batches to prioritize quality over volume.
