About SERENE

The Concept

SERENE is a Georgia-based atelier specializing in premium loungewear. Founded on the principle of intentional living, we create garments that bridge the gap between private comfort and sartorial elegance.

Craftsmanship

Every piece is designed and produced entirely in Georgia. We collaborate with local master seamstresses ensuring absolute oversight of every stitch, seam, and silhouette.

Materials

We work exclusively with high-grade silk and velvet, selected for structural integrity, superior drape, and tactile longevity.

Slow Production

We operate on a slow-release model, producing in limited batches to maintain the highest standards of quality and to minimize waste.
